# Gamification Architecture

## 1. Purpose of Gamification
Gamification should increase motivation, sustain engagement, and communicate learner progress. It should feel meaningful and integrated with learning, not superficial or gimmicky. The system should support both intrinsic motivation and structured reward loops.

## 2. Core Gamification Entities
- experience points
- level progression
- badges
- streaks
- achievements
- milestones
- unlocks
- leaderboards

## 3. Reward Design Principles
Rewards should be:
- meaningful,
- understandable,
- achievable,
- and tied to real progress.

## 4. Progression Loops
The gamification system should reinforce behaviors such as:
- completing lessons,
- solving problems,
- revisiting concepts,
- collaborating with squads,
- and maintaining consistency.

## 5. Reward Rules Engine
A rules engine should calculate rewards based on events emitted by the platform. This enables content authors and product teams to tune reward logic without changing core code.

## 6. Fairness and Avoidance of Exploitation
The design should prevent trivial exploitation such as reward farming. Reward conditions should be tied to quality and context, not just activity volume.

## 7. Personalization
The reward experience should be personalized based on learner behavior and preferences. Some learners may respond better to badges and streaks, while others prefer mastery milestones and narrative progression.

## 8. Analytics Integration
Gamification events should feed analytics so the platform can understand which growth patterns are effective and which are not.
