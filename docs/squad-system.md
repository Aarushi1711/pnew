# Squad System Design

## 1. Purpose of Squads
The squad system enables learners to form groups for collaborative study, peer accountability, and shared mission progress. It should support a flexible social layer that can scale from friend circles to structured study cohorts.

## 2. Squad Model
A squad should contain:
- a name and description,
- members with roles,
- shared goals or missions,
- chat or discussion context,
- and progress visibility settings.

## 3. Membership Model
Membership states should include:
- invited,
- active,
- pending,
- blocked,
- and left.

## 4. Collaboration Features
Initial features may include:
- shared mission participation,
- streak visibility,
- team-based achievements,
- discussion threads,
- and peer encouragement.

## 5. Permissions and Safety
The system must support safe collaboration by defining:
- who can invite members,
- who can post content,
- who can view progress,
- and who can manage group settings.

## 6. Social Graph Considerations
The squad system should be designed around a social graph so it can later support:
- clubs,
- mentorship circles,
- tournaments,
- and community journeys.

## 7. Event-Driven Squad Logic
Squad actions should trigger events such as:
- member joined,
- mission shared,
- achievement unlocked,
- and team milestone reached.

## 8. Privacy and Moderation
The system should protect learner privacy and define moderation policies for group interaction and content visibility.
